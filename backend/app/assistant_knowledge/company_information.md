# Soko-Link — Customer Support Knowledge Base

## 1. Platform Overview

**Soko-Link** is a multi-participant digital marketplace that connects
buyers with trusted sellers, supported by delivery personnel,
intermediaries, and platform administrators.

Soko-Link enables customers to:
- Discover and compare products across multiple sellers
- Review verified seller profiles and business information
- Communicate directly with sellers
- Place, track, and manage orders
- Complete purchases with confidence

Soko-Link enables sellers to:
- Establish a professional online presence
- List and manage products, pricing, and inventory
- Reach a wider customer base
- Track sales performance and fulfillment

**Platform goal:** bring buyers and sellers together in one integrated
digital commerce environment that makes buying easy and helps
businesses grow.

---

## 2. Who Uses Soko-Link

| Role | Summary |
|---|---|
| Buyers | Customers who discover, order, and receive products |
| Sellers | Businesses/individuals who list and sell products |
| Delivery Personnel | Transport orders from sellers to buyers |
| Middlemen / Intermediaries | Facilitate transactions in some workflows |
| Platform Administrators | Oversee platform operations, disputes, and integrity |

---

## 3. Buyers

Buyers can:
- Search and discover products
- Browse product categories and listings
- Compare sellers, pricing, and product details
- View verified seller profiles and business information
- Place and manage orders
- Track order and delivery status
- Communicate with sellers about products and orders
- Manage their account and view purchase history
- Request customer support

---

## 4. Sellers

Sellers can:
- Create and manage a seller account and profile
- List, edit, and manage products
- Set pricing and product availability
- Manage inventory
- Receive and manage customer orders
- Coordinate fulfillment
- Communicate with buyers and resolve inquiries
- Monitor sales performance and track fulfillment
- Manage their business presence and account settings

---

## 5. Delivery Personnel

Delivery personnel transport orders between sellers and buyers. Their
responsibilities may include:
- Accepting delivery assignments
- Collecting products from sellers
- Transporting and delivering products to buyers
- Updating delivery status
- Confirming successful delivery
- Reporting delivery problems

> ⚠️ **Agent rule:** Actual delivery workflows are determined by the
> live Soko-Link delivery system. Always use current platform/order
> data (not this document) when answering questions about a *specific*
> delivery's status or timing.

---

## 6. Middlemen / Intermediaries

Some transactions involve intermediaries who help coordinate deals
between buyers and sellers. Depending on the workflow, they may:
- Connect buyers with sellers
- Facilitate product sourcing or negotiations
- Coordinate communication, logistics, or fulfillment
- Support other marketplace activities

> ⚠️ **Agent rule:** Do not assume every intermediary performs the same
> function — their role varies by transaction. If a customer asks about
> a specific intermediary's responsibilities, check platform data/tools
> rather than guessing.

---

## 7. Platform Administrators

Administrators can:
- Monitor marketplace and sales activity
- Manage users, roles, and access permissions
- Review seller verification and business information
- Support transactions, handle complaints and disputes
- Maintain platform integrity and smooth operation

---

## 8. How a Transaction Flows

A transaction may follow different paths depending on whether an
intermediary is involved:

- **Buyer → Seller → Delivery Personnel → Buyer** (standard)
- **Buyer → Intermediary → Seller → Delivery Personnel → Buyer**
- **Buyer → Seller → Intermediary → Delivery Personnel → Buyer**

> ⚠️ **Agent rule:** Do not assume a particular workflow for a given
> order unless it's confirmed by platform data or the knowledge base.
> If unsure which path applies, say so and offer to check the specific
> order rather than guessing.

---

## 9. Business Model

Soko-Link operates as a **multi-seller marketplace**: independent
sellers offer products through one shared platform, which provides
infrastructure for product visibility, transaction processing,
customer engagement, and operational management.

**Value for buyers:** convenient shopping, product visibility, ability
to compare sellers/products, access to seller information, a
structured purchasing environment.

**Value for sellers:** marketplace presence, wider customer reach,
online product/order management, customer communication tools, sales
performance visibility.

## 11. Marketplace Coordination & Access Rules

Soko-Link coordinates product discovery, seller discovery,
buyer-seller communication, transactions, orders, deliveries, status
updates, support, and disputes across all participants.

> ⚠️ **Agent rule — access control:** Different participants have
> different permissions. Only provide a customer with information they
> are authorized to receive (e.g., don't share another seller's
> internal data, another buyer's order details, or a delivery
> person's personal contact info).

---

## 12. Support Agent Guardrails (Summary)

1. Use **live platform/order data**, not this document, for anything
   order- or delivery-specific (status, ETA, current price, stock).
2. Never assume a transaction workflow or an intermediary's role —
   confirm via platform data when specifics are asked.
3. Only share information the requesting user is authorized to see.
4. If the answer isn't covered here or in live data, say so and offer
   to escalate to a human agent rather than guessing.
